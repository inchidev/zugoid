**Media** in the rail is a library of photos and videos, apart from any conversation. Items are listed by when they were taken, newest first, grouped by month. Open one to see it full size or play it.

The list in Media has three views:

- **My library**: everything you imported. Only you see an item until you share it.
- **Shared in this Space**: items others in the Space shared with it.
- **From conversations**: photos and videos sent in conversations you are in. They stay in their conversations; **Open in conversation** goes to the message.

## Import

Choose **Import** in **My library**, then one of:

- **Photos and videos**: pick files from your device.
- **A folder**: pick a folder; Media takes the photos and videos in it and in its subfolders.

Media takes JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, HEIC, AVIF, MP4, MOV and WebM. Everything is read in your browser.

The time a photo was taken comes from the photo itself (EXIF), or else from the file's date.

An import can stop and start again. Files already in your library are recognised by name, size and time taken, and skipped without being sent again. Files over 95 MB are sent in parts.

Your browser draws a small preview of each item while importing. A format it cannot draw, such as HEIC outside Safari, is imported without a preview and opens as the original.

Media counts toward the Space's storage limit, like files in conversations and Tasks. Importing takes the **Attach files** permission.

## Share and delete

Open one of your items and choose **Share with Space** to show it to everyone in the Space, or **Make private** to keep it to yourself again. **Delete** removes the item and its file; it cannot be undone. Only the person who imported an item can share or delete it.

A Space that holds Media items cannot be deleted until they are removed.

The Owner can hide Media from the sidebar in **Settings → Appearance**.
